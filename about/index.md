Marta Forsberg is a Swedish-Polish, Berlin-based artist whose work constantly shifts between compositional discipline and emotional resonance. Delving into themes of language and human memory with a strong focus on archival research, Forsberg often experiments with the interplay of human and synthetic voices. She’s released a number of albums on labels like Warm Winters Ltd., XKatedral and Superpang, and collaborated across the realms of theatre, dance and performance.
