First newsletter!

Dear friends,
Feels really good to be in touch like this, my first newsletter, wow! I will try to sum up what's going on, looking back and ahead, share my favorite records - stuff like that. Thanks so much for reading! ♡

![Photo:Yola Moschitz](/assets/images/260210_konzert_c_yola_moschitz_011.jpg)
*Picture from concert in Graz at Forum Stadtpark, back in February. Photo: Yola Moschitz*

My record "Archaeology of Intimacy" has been out for a year now, and its been a wonderful time performing this music around Europe. Now I'm playing two last shows of this year:
ꨄ︎ Hamburg on November 5th, at Stubnitz as part of the concert series Upstream. Alex Zhang Hungtai and Tripolar Ghost are also playing, amazing evening!
ꨄ︎ Berlin on November 18th, at Sowieso. Sharing the evening with super star string trio Maccabee–Marino–Walker, will be great!!
There has been a couple of fantastic compilations released this fall, which include tracks of mine, from two of my favorite labels ♡ Synthetic Water Music at mappa and Superpang: World Tour Vol.2 at Superpang ♡ full of wonderful music, have a listen!

![mappa-Superpang](/assets/images/mappa-Superpang.png)

I'm also working on a new archival project at Föreningsarkivet Västernorrland. Together with sound artists Louise Ölund and Jim Hoult we're invited to explore the sounding part of the archive, consisting mostly of interviews made with people who were active in the workers movement in the north of Sweden during the first half of the 1900s. Super exciting!! 

![Kubikenborg](/assets/images/Fotografi, Kubikenborgs brädgård, sekelskiftet.jpg)
*Workers at Kubikenborg wood factory, around year 1900*

And then the thing that occupies me the most these last months - I'm working on a new record <3 it feels wonderful and scary as always, this time I wanna have more friends being part of the process. First one to join is my longtime friend and support node, and now also my manager, Adam Badí Donoval - helping me figure out the path forward and keeping an ear to the music I make. I feel unbelievably grateful for this! <3

Lastly - favorite music, listening on repeat: "Dalok" by Adela Mede & Štefan Szabó, "I Love My Computer" by Ninajirachi and "Polski Sklep" by Krenz ♡
Thanks again for reading, and lots of love!
/Marta ♡♡♡
